# Design references

Real sites, small organisations, nothing that reads as a template. Study them the way a designer studies a clippings file: screenshot two or three that are close to the client's kind of organisation (`pnpm shot <url> <url>`, then open `.artifacts/refs/*.png`), name what each one does that this client could use, and name what you refuse. Adapt the thinking, never the site: their copy, logo, imagery and structure belong to them.

Fonts listed are what the site serves; the arrow gives the Google Fonts stand-in when the original is a paid face.

## Church / faith

- https://saint.church/ — black on white, one red; the hero is nothing but very large rotating headlines in a grotesk; a four-item nav (Church / What's On / Join In / Give). Reads like a music venue, not a parish. Ginto + GT Alpina italic → Instrument Sans or Schibsted Grotesk + Instrument Serif italic.
- https://gemeinschaft-immanuel.de/ — type-only hero of three stacked words; a sans, a serif and a mono plus green/olive/yellow/salmon tokens carry the identity instead of photography. Aeonik + Instrument Serif + JetBrains Mono → Manrope + Instrument Serif + JetBrains Mono.
- https://www.stiftadmont.at/ — one family in three cuts (IBM Plex Sans / Serif / Sans Condensed) and a single gold token; text-led hero, then four pillars. All three cuts are on Google Fonts.
- https://www.nidarosdomen.no/ — a serif in three optical sizes against a plain sans; opening hours before any imagery. Kepler + Basic Sans → Fraunces (opsz axis) or Newsreader + Instrument Sans.
- https://www.kloster-fahr.ch/ — the homepage keyed to the monastic hours, each with its own image and one line. Brygada 1918 + Open Sans, both on Google Fonts.

## Nonprofit / community

- https://joycetower.ie/ — black, white, one electric blue; a literary quote as the hero beside a line drawing. Apris + Poppins → Fraunces or Young Serif + Poppins.
- https://brooklyn.org/ — type-only hero ("Brooklyn Backs Brooklyn") in a grotesk with a condensed companion for labels; coral accent; no hero image. → Archivo + Archivo Narrow or Barlow + Barlow Condensed.
- https://www.deveron-projects.com/ — seasonal, place-based hero with garden photography; sections read like a town noticeboard. Next.js on Vercel. Founders Grotesk → Public Sans or Instrument Sans.
- https://tudortrust.org.uk/ — warm flat colour blocks (cream, peach, plum, lime, lilac) as the structure; a condensed cut for labels. → Figtree or Instrument Sans + Archivo Narrow.
- https://veluxfonden.dk/ — serif for nearly all running text, a sans only for UI; hero sentence plus one documentary photo. → Source Serif 4 or Newsreader + Instrument Sans.
- https://ternerlabs.org/ — tagline-only hero on cream with pastel section fills; geometric sans heads, book serif body, no stock photos. → Manrope + Source Serif 4 or Lora.
- https://www.skyhighfarm.org — one grotesk in two weights, documentary farm photography, the about page as a dated timeline. → Archivo or Instrument Sans.

## Small business: café, restaurant, shop, farm, hotel

- https://luca.restaurant — condensed grotesk headlines against a serif italic for dish names; an editorial grid of features instead of a hero slab; photos small and square. → Barlow Condensed + Instrument Serif italic.
- https://www.lenclume.co.uk — one neutral sans at small sizes on cream; the photography talks; menus as plain typographic lists. → Inter Tight or Geist.
- https://www.thedaughter.ca — one family at every size; all-caps hero; the shop grid directly under it. Venue and retail on one page.
- https://www.dirtyvine.com — high-contrast display serif for the tagline with a mono for support; product photos plus illustrated tiles in earth tones. Swear Display + DM Mono → Fraunces (soft axis) or Instrument Serif + DM Mono.
- https://www.brau.wine — no hero image: name, mission line, four collections as typographic blocks, a terroir map. → Inter or Archivo + EB Garamond or Libre Caslon.
- https://michecoffee.com — a soft display serif with a plain Times body; leads with an art carousel. → Fraunces or Newsreader + a system serif.
- https://www.suzanehotel.com — Manrope everywhere plus one script accent for the name; cream ground, single green; rooms as four named tiers.
- https://www.heartandsoilflowers.com/ — whites, blacks, neutrals only; naturalistic photography carries it. → Libre Caslon Text + Instrument Sans.

## Gym, climbing, sports club, yoga

- https://skyting.com — sans for UI against a high-contrast serif for class names; the live schedule is the hero. → Inter + Fraunces or Instrument Serif.
- https://innerflow.es — one grotesk and its mono; big lower-case statement hero; class list as plain text. → Inter or Manrope + DM Mono.
- https://sollertennisclub.com — one page, almost no copy: full-bleed hero, a crest, one drone shot, one CTA. Next.js.
- https://www.bolderclub.com — cobalt as the only colour; heavy display headlines; old sports-club lettering; jokey copy. → Outfit + Archivo Black or Anton.
- https://blocshop.com — wide heavy extended headlines over black and white with close-crop climbing photos. → Plus Jakarta Sans + Big Shoulders or Archivo (wdth).
- https://www.the-font.co.uk — dark ground, white text, extended grotesk caps for section heads; locations as address-and-hours blocks. → Archivo Expanded or Big Shoulders.

## Design studio, freelancer

- https://studiobruch.com — work rows each introduced by a stacked three-line headline broken with pipes; slab serif against a grotesk; no colour, all rhythm. → Zilla Slab or Roboto Slab + a grotesk.
- https://jameswalsh.studio — a numbered single-column list of full-width project photos with a plain title under each; one typeface, one weight; light and dark. Next.js + Special Gothic (Google Fonts).
- https://www.tillmannfranzen.com — a first-person paragraph, then a vertical stack of one-photo project blocks; a list, not a grid. → Archivo + Archivo Narrow.
- https://heurebleue.studio — a sentence as hero, then six photos in a staggered grid mixing orientations on white. → Inter Tight or Instrument Sans + a light display.
- https://daylit.studio/ — cream and navy with a light/dark toggle; a credentials row; six project cards; a FAQ. Atkinson Hyperlegible + Newsreader, both on Google Fonts.
- https://satoshiwatanabe.org — the work index as a numbered reverse-chronological table (number / client / campaign / year).

## Youth, education

- https://lesniklubjasanek.cz — large centred name as hero, text in blocks with a lot of white, staff portraits as the only photography. → a geometric sans + IBM Plex Mono.
- https://www.katholino.de — extra-bold rounded display as graphic taglines, big figures, vibrant colour blocks, candid playground photography. → Bricolage Grotesque, Sora ExtraBold or Fredoka.
- https://primer.com — a deliberately flat type scale so nothing shouts; a serif accent, grotesk body; a Mondrian-ish colour grid. → Instrument Serif or Fraunces + Inter or Geist.
- https://www.korowa.vic.edu.au — the headline copy carries the page; italic serif as emphasis inside a DM Sans sentence; earth-tone photography instead of school blue. → DM Sans + Instrument Serif italic.
- https://www.bryanston.co.uk — aerial campus hero, then four age-band tiles as the navigation. → Libre Caslon Text or Cormorant.
